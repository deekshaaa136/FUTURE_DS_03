Marketing Funnel Analysis & Dashboard

FutureInterns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

Task 3: Marketing Funnel Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project is completed as part of my Data Science & Analytics Internship at FutureInterns.

The objective of this task is to analyze an e-commerce marketing funnel and understand how visitors move through different stages of the customer journey, from visiting the website to making a purchase.

The analysis focuses on identifying conversion patterns, evaluating marketing channel performance, and understanding which channels contribute to better purchases and revenue.

The final output is a client-ready dashboard created using Excel/WPS Office.

---

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the e-commerce marketing funnel.
- Understand visitor and purchase behavior.
- Calculate the purchase conversion rate.
- Compare different marketing channels.
- Analyze revenue generated by each marketing channel.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing channels.
- Visualize important business KPIs.
- Generate meaningful business insights.
- Provide recommendations based on the analysis.

---

🔄 E-commerce Conversion Funnel

The conversion funnel shows how users move through the customer journey and helps identify where potential customers are being lost before completing a purchase.

The funnel analysis helps answer questions such as:

- How many visitors entered the funnel?
- How many visitors converted into purchasers?
- What percentage of visitors completed a purchase?
- Where can the conversion process be improved?

---

📈 Marketing Channel Analysis

The project compares different marketing channels to understand their performance.

The dashboard includes:

Marketing Channel Performance

A comparison of the performance of different marketing channels based on visitor and purchase activity.

Revenue by Marketing Channel

Shows the revenue generated by each marketing channel and helps identify channels contributing the most to overall revenue.

Conversion Rate by Marketing Channel

Compares the purchase conversion rate across marketing channels to identify channels that are more effective at converting visitors into customers.

---

💡 Business Insights

The analysis provides insights into customer conversion behavior and marketing channel performance.

By comparing visitors, purchases, conversion rates, and revenue, it becomes possible to identify which marketing channels are performing effectively and which channels may require improvement.

The funnel also helps highlight the gap between the number of visitors and the number of completed purchases, providing an opportunity to improve the customer journey and increase conversions.

---

💭 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

- Focus marketing efforts on channels with strong conversion performance.
- Investigate channels with high visitor numbers but comparatively low purchase conversion.
- Improve the customer journey to reduce visitor drop-off.
- Monitor conversion rates regularly for each marketing channel.
- Allocate marketing resources based on both revenue and conversion performance.
- Continue tracking funnel performance to identify changes in customer behavior.
- Use data-driven decisions to improve overall marketing effectiveness.
